---
title: A new hope for one who is lost
date: 2026-09-05T20:00:00.000Z
excerpt: For Hermi, and for a hope that still holds in a world that gives sorrow.
---

I always wanted to have my own website, and it seems I did such an extravagant site that seems soooo bougie that I can't describe. But that one person in my life, Hermi, who is the one who truly understands me, makes me truly feel like I can be myself — that makes it worth it. I also seem to have something to hope in this evil world that gives nothing but sorrow, and for those gifts I give thanks.
