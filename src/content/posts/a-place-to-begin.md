This is the first note on oboros.space.

Replace this file, or add another markdown document beside it, whenever you want the journal to grow. The cover stays the same. The writing does not.

Keep the frontmatter at the top. Everything below it is the page.

```
src/content/posts/your-title.md
```

Then rebuild, or save while the dev server is running, and the journal will pick it up.
